! Title: UK food ordering blocklist
! Description: Blocks takeaway and convenience delivery ordering in the UK:
!   the big marketplaces AND the platforms local takeaways use for their own
!   branded websites and apps. For anyone who wants to stop ordering food.
! Homepage: https://github.com/myredroom/uk-food-ordering-blocklist
! License: CC0-1.0
!
! Format: ||domain^ blocks the domain and every subdomain
! (Pi-hole v6, AdGuard Home, uBlock Origin).
!
! Approach: block PLATFORMS, not individual takeaways. A takeaway with its own
! web address almost always takes the order through one of the platforms
! below, so one platform entry covers hundreds of takeaways.
! Never add payment processors (Stripe, Adyen, PayPal...): that would break
! all online shopping.
